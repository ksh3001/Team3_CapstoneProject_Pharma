## Problem (one paragraph)

NTG must shorten evidence-reconciliation time for batch-review readiness, PV intake support, and supply-shortage options under a board lead-time target—while Quality, Safety and Supply accountable humans retain final authority. Today’s brownfield extracts and unsafe starter patterns create conflict, rework and side-effect risk; measured cycle-time baselines are still Unknown.

## Governing outcome (experiment)

Qualify and test a **fail-closed, offline-capable evidence-assist** that improves citation, conflict detection and option drafting—sequenced after / with master-data and rules work—without autonomous regulated decisions. Upgrade from `hypothesis` to `decision-ready` only after Measure evidence lands.

## Non-goals (vision level)

Not an autonomous release, PV adjudication, or allocation engine. Not a vendor/model lock-in. Not a full Prompt 09 waste workshop substitute.
